# AgentOS — brag plan

**What it is:** A local-first command center that turns one outcome into a coordinated crew of AI agents you can watch work.
**For:** Hackers, students and small teams who want multi-agent results without cloud lock-in.
**Sets it apart:** Crew handoff (each agent builds on the last), a live pipeline you can watch, zero pip dependencies, runs on Ollama.
**Most impressive claim:** One Python file, zero dependencies: a full multi-agent mission control.
**Visual hook:** A single lonely chat answer, then four agents lighting up in a chain.
**Real UI shown:** Mission Control composer (real template text), the live pipeline (Scout → Miller → Juniper → Clover), the mission detail's final answer, and the Export .md action.
**Tone:** `default` leaning polished: dark, violet→cyan gradient, Inter + JetBrains Mono, soft transitions.
**Share line:** "One outcome. A crew of AI agents. AgentOS runs a whole team of agents on your laptop, and you watch every handoff live."
