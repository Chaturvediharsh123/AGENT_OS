# AgentOS: brag v2 (60s)

**Angle:** "Stop asking one AI." The video shows the real app running a four-agent crew from start to finish: describe, dispatch, watch the handoff, inspect, compare, export, search.
**Source:** every app shot is a snapshot of the real AgentOS UI. It was captured from a private instance (port 8090, throwaway DB) seeded with demo-provider missions. The camera pans and zooms inside a browser frame; typing, clicks, pipeline progress and toasts are animated on top of the real markup.
